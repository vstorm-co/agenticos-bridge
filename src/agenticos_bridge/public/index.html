YOUR CHOICE

One account flow.
Two ways to connect.

01

Continue with AgenticOS

Use our shared provider app. The bridge handles OAuth consent and token renewal, then returns your tokens to your AgenticOS instance.

02

Use your own credentials

Configure your organization’s provider app in AgenticOS. Your instance connects directly, without using this bridge.

Where your data goes

Your AgenticOS↔OAuth bridge↔Google / Microsoft / Slack

The bridge temporarily processes authorization codes and tokens. It does not store your account tokens or receive your email, files, calendars or chat content. Your instance makes those requests directly to the provider.

Read the privacy policy →

Permissions stay explicit.

You choose the connected products and requested permissions in AgenticOS. Read access is the starting point; writes require a separate choice. Your provider may also require administrator consent or app approval.

The bridge is a trusted OAuth service: its process handles tokens in memory during exchange and refresh. Learn about that boundary in our security documentation.
