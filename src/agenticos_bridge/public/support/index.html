Before you connect

Your AgenticOS build must include native connected accounts and be configured to use this bridge. The integration is tracked in AgenticOS #2805; it may not yet be included in your released version. Shared sign-in also requires the provider app and your deployment to be enabled.

Connect an account

In AgenticOS, open Chat → Preferences → Your accounts, or Settings → Profile. Choose a provider, Continue with AgenticOS, the products you need and whether to include write access. Organization administrators can connect shared accounts in Vault. If shared sign-in is unavailable, use your organization’s own provider credentials.

Repair a connection

Use Reconnect on the affected account in AgenticOS. Keep the original account and connection method. If the provider requires administrator approval, ask your Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra or Slack workspace administrator. Your provider may also revoke grants or require a fresh consent.

Disconnect or revoke access

Remove the account in your AgenticOS instance. To revoke the provider grant itself, visit Google account connections, Microsoft My Apps, or your Slack workspace’s installed-app settings. Revoking a shared app may affect other connections using that app.

Get help

Contact info@vstorm.co or open a non-sensitive issue in GitHub. Never include real tokens, authorization codes, client secrets or configuration files in a public issue.

Privacy and security

Privacy requests: gdpr@vstorm.co. Report vulnerabilities through GitHub private reporting. Read our privacy policy and terms of service.

Request shared access

The shared service is invitation-only. Email dev@vstorm.co with your organization, exact AgenticOS URL, intended providers and incident contact. We review each request before issuing a time-limited deployment key. Own-provider credentials remain available without approval.
