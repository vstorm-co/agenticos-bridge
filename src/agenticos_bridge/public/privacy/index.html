Privacy policy

This policy describes the AgenticOS Bridge service at bridge-agenticos.testapp.ovh. It is separate from the AgenticOS instance you connect.

Who operates this service

The service is operated by VSTORM Sp. z o.o., Wrocław, Poland. Contact info@vstorm.co for service questions and gdpr@vstorm.co for privacy requests. The Vstorm privacy policy provides the company’s full controller details and information about handling correspondence and privacy rights.

What the bridge processes

When you choose Continue with AgenticOS, the bridge processes the provider name, selected OAuth scopes, your destination instance’s callback address, PKCE proof, authorization code and provider-issued access and refresh tokens. This is necessary to connect the account and renew its authorization. OAuth codes, tokens and handoff tickets are credentials and may grant access to personal data.

The service receives ordinary network metadata, including the requesting IP address, as part of delivering and protecting HTTPS connections. It does not need or request email messages, document contents, calendar entries or Slack message contents.

Reviewed deployment access

Shared access is invitation-only. To apply, email dev@vstorm.co. We store deployment access-key hashes, the approved instance origin and providers, operator contact, agreement reference, issue/expiry dates and revocation status. These records support access control and incident communication; they do not contain account tokens. Access records and backups are not automatically deleted; contact us about the retention applicable to your reviewed deployment.

Storage and retention

Account tokens and codes exist transiently in bridge process memory while a request is handled. The bridge has no user-token database and does not persist request or response bodies. Tokens and encrypted refresh receipts are returned to your instance and stored in that instance’s owner-bound Vault. Shared provider application secrets and the bridge’s encryption key are persistent server configuration; they are not your account tokens.

OAuth request-query logging is disabled for this service. Operational logs record service startup and failures without intentionally recording credential payloads. The service has no user-content archive, analytics tracking, advertising cookies or browser account session. If you contact support, your correspondence is handled under Vstorm’s general privacy policy.

Who receives data

The provider you choose—Google, Microsoft or Slack—receives the OAuth requests. Tokens are returned only to the AgenticOS deployment whose connection flow you started and proved with PKCE. Vstorm’s service infrastructure processes requests to operate the bridge. The bridge operator is a trusted party because tokens pass through its process memory.

Your AgenticOS deployment requests authorized work data directly from the provider. Its administrator controls its storage, models, tools, sharing and retention. Review that deployment’s policies before connecting: this bridge policy does not cover or guarantee the behavior of arbitrary self-hosted instances.

Use of Google data

AgenticOS Bridge’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The bridge uses OAuth credentials only to provide the account connection and token renewal you request. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

The bridge does not send account credentials to AI models. Any AI processing of email, files or other work data happens in your chosen AgenticOS deployment, under its configuration and permissions.

Security limitations

A compromise of the bridge or its host could expose credentials while they pass through memory, allowing access within their granted permissions. No service can promise zero risk. Revoking a deployment key stops subsequent bridge operations, but does not retract already issued provider tokens; revoke the application grant at the provider as well.

Disconnecting and deletion

Disconnect the account in AgenticOS to remove the local connection. You can also revoke the shared application’s access in your Google Account, Microsoft account or Slack workspace settings. Local disconnection does not automatically revoke the provider grant. The bridge has no stored account record or token database to delete; contact your deployment administrator to delete local data and Vstorm for data you sent directly to support.

Your choices and rights

You can use your own OAuth application credentials instead of this bridge. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw consent where applicable and complain to your supervisory authority. Send privacy requests to gdpr@vstorm.co; the Vstorm policy explains the available contact routes.

Policy changes

Material changes will be reflected in this page’s update date. The open-source repository records changes to the service and this policy.
