Terms of service

AgenticOS Bridge connects accounts you are authorized to use to an AgenticOS deployment you choose.

The service

VSTORM Sp. z o.o. operates this shared OAuth service. It exchanges authorization codes and renews tokens for supported providers. It does not host your AgenticOS deployment or act as a general proxy for your work data. Provider availability depends on application configuration, verification, provider terms and your organization’s policies.

Access by invitation

Shared access requires an individually reviewed request to dev@vstorm.co and a deployment key issued by Vstorm. Keys are limited to an approved instance, providers and validity period. Do not share or resell keys. Review applicable service terms and organizational agreements with Vstorm before production use. Approval does not guarantee security or provider acceptance. Own-provider credentials remain an independent option.

Your responsibilities

Use the service only for accounts and deployments you are authorized to connect. Check the destination instance and requested permissions before consenting. Protect your own deployment and credentials. Do not use the service to obtain unauthorized access, impersonate another user, evade provider restrictions, overload the service or compromise another deployment.

Provider rules and privacy

Your use of Google, Microsoft, Slack and other third-party services remains subject to their terms and permissions. Your organization may need to approve access. Our privacy policy describes bridge processing; the destination deployment has its own data responsibilities.

Availability and changes

The shared service is provided on an as-available basis. Features and access may be restricted or suspended for security, abuse prevention, maintenance or provider policy requirements. There is no service-level commitment under this public service notice; any separately agreed contract remains separate. You can operate with your own provider credentials to avoid dependence on the shared bridge.
